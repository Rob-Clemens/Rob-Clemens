Hi, I'm Rob Clemens
Skills Development Lead for Planet Research Data Commons at the Australian Research Data Commons (ARDC). I help researchers build the skills to find, use and share research data well.

What I work on
DReSA: I manage the development and maintenance of the Digital Research Skills Australasia web application, a shared catalogue of training events, materials and trainers for the Australasian research community, built on the open-source TeSS platform.

ARDC AI educators: five AI learning assistants for the dataspaces domain (an educator and four coaches), free to use on DReSA. App definitions, corpus recipe and setup guide: au-research/AI-educators.

From sound to species maps: an ecoacoustics and spatial modelling use case. With the teams at Open Ecoacoustics and EcoCommons, I am developing an end-to-end research workflow for the Powerful Owl in southeast Queensland:
Passive acoustic sensors record in the field, and the recordings are uploaded to Ecosounds.

The audio is processed into species detections, which members of the public validate through Call Detective.
The validated detections feed spatial analyses: predicting likely nest sites from AlphaEarth data, then estimating occupancy with a hierarchical model that combines observation records and acoustic data.
