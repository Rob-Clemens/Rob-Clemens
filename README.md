### Hi, I'm Rob Clemens

Skills Development Lead for Planet Research Data Commons at the
[Australian Research Data Commons (ARDC)](https://ardc.edu.au). I help
researchers build the skills to find, use and share research data well.

#### What I work on

- **[DReSA](https://dresa.org.au)**: I manage the development and maintenance
  of the Digital Research Skills Australasia web application, a shared
  catalogue of training events, materials and trainers for the Australasian
  research community, built on the open-source
  [TeSS](https://github.com/ElixirTeSS/TeSS) platform.
- **[ARDC AI educators](https://dresa.org.au/educators)**: five AI learning
  assistants for the dataspaces domain (an educator and four coaches), free to
  use on DReSA. App definitions, corpus recipe and setup guide:
  [au-research/AI-educators](https://github.com/au-research/AI-educators).
- **From sound to species maps: an ecoacoustics and spatial modelling use
  case.** With the teams at [Open Ecoacoustics](https://openecoacoustics.org/)
  and [EcoCommons](https://www.ecocommons.org.au/), I am developing an
  end-to-end research workflow for the Powerful Owl in southeast Queensland:
  1. Passive acoustic sensors record in the field, and the recordings are
     uploaded to [Ecosounds](https://www.ecosounds.org/).
  2. The audio is processed into species detections, which members of the
     public validate through [Call Detective](https://powerful-owl.calldetective.org/).
  3. The validated detections feed spatial analyses: predicting likely nest
     sites from [AlphaEarth data](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaearth-foundations-helps-map-our-planet-in-unprecedented-detail/), then estimating occupancy with a
     hierarchical model that combines observation records and acoustic data.
